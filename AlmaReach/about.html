Our mission

We are redefining how development teams access and use institutional intelligence.

By harnessing cutting-edge technology, we automate data retrieval and analysis at a scale and depth never before possible. Our platform draws from diverse data sources to transform limited, fragmented information into clear, strategic insight.

The result: development teams equipped with unprecedented visibility, sharper strategy, and the confidence to pursue fundraising opportunities with clarity and conviction.

Our story

Born Out of Frustration and Conviction

Inside the Development Office

Strategy Held Back by Data

Working inside Development Offices, we saw firsthand how ambitious fundraising strategies were being held back by one persistent constraint: incomplete, outdated, and fragmented data.

Out of DateEmployment records were years out of date.
Out of SightAlumni had moved industries, founded companies and risen into leadership roles, yet none of it was reflected in the data.
Out of ReachRoutine prospecting tasks became hours of manual detective work.
The cost

Extraordinary People, Flying Under the Radar

Entire cohorts of alumni were effectively invisible. Not because anyone had failed, but because at an institutional scale, tracking tens or hundreds of thousands of individuals manually is simply impossible. Even the most skilled researchers cannot keep pace with the speed of modern careers.

Without comprehensive current data, critical opportunities were missed. Major gift prospects went unidentified. Potential mentors remained undiscovered. Alumni networks appeared smaller and less dynamic than they truly were.

The question

We Kept Asking a Simple Question

Why can’t we do better?

We searched for a solution. None existed.

The answer

So We Built One

Today, Development teams are navigating increasingly complex campaigns whilst relying on fragmented enrichment tools, static datasets, and opaque scoring systems. AlmaReach replaces that patchwork approach with a unified, automated platform powered by AI.

AlmaReach isn’t just another tool, it’s a new foundation for institutional intelligence.

How We Research

01

Search

Starting from any anchor you give us, such as a name and organisation, an email address or a LinkedIn profile, we search accessible public sources.

02

Cross-Check

Key facts are cross-referenced across a diverse array of sources, and every profile carries an identity-confidence score.

03

Weigh the Evidence

Findings are weighed as a specialist researcher would weigh them, before any rating or recommendation is made.

04

Ground & Cite

Every estimate is grounded in empirical results, and every claim is given with its source.

Our principles

What Distinguishes AlmaReach

Scale

Unlike manual researchers, we use modern automation tools to provide enriched profiles and insights for many thousands of alumni, ensuring that no stone goes unturned in the Advancement pipeline.

Transparency

All scoring frameworks and modelling approaches are clearly documented. With no black-box outputs, institutions can interpret exactly what our outputs mean for their fundraising efforts.

Privacy

We hold ourselves to the highest standards regarding data sharing and privacy. No processing is ever conducted without documented lawful basis. Security is paramount. See our Privacy Policy

Evidence

Every claim comes from accessible public sources, and citations are always given, so your team can check any finding for itself.

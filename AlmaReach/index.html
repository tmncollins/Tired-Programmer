What we offer

Our Services

AlmaReach sets a new standard for Development. Every claim comes from accessible public sources, and citations are always given.

Service 01

Profile Enrichment & Rating

Give us anything that identifies a person: a name and organisation, an email address, a LinkedIn profile or any other anchor. From that, we build a full profile: current role and career history, directorships, trusteeships and philanthropic activity. Each person is then rated for giving capacity, a recommended first ask, and more, with an identity-confidence score and every source cited.

Best for: in-depth wealth screening, major gift prospecting, briefing notes.

Pricing
Service 02

List & Database Creation

We discover new individuals for you: for example, Directors, Board Members and Partners at companies or institutions you have worked with, or people drawn from other sources to suit your needs. We then enrich and rate each person using Service 01. We can also match and structure patchy records into a clean database ready for your CRM.

Best for: building new prospect lists, reconnecting with corporate partners and sponsors, and (re)building an alumni database that has been neglected.

Pricing
Service 03

Cross-Referencing

We match and cross-reference people across separate lists and systems, such as event lists, CRM exports and external datasets. Fuzzy name matching handles records in different formats, nicknames, maiden names, typos and missing fields, then each match is confirmed as the same individual, so duplicates are resolved and your records join up.

Best for: merging databases, removing duplicates, and matching outside lists against your database (e.g. Rich Lists, Honours).

Pricing
Service 04

Basic Wealth Screening

A quick, light-touch screen of whole databases. We search for your individuals across a smaller set of aggregated sources, including Companies House and other ownership directories, to identify which of them appear there. This does not result in a rating for each person, and is in much less depth than Service 01, but it shows your team where to look first. It is an identification tool.

Best for: finding some new prospects across your whole alumni or supporter base.

Pricing
Service 05

Custom

Research shaped around a specific question, such as a campaign, a region, a sector or an event guest list. Tell us what you need and we'll scope it with you.

Best for: one-off projects that don't fit a standard service.
